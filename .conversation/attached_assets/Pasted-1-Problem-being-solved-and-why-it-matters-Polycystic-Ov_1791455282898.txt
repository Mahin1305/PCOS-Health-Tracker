1)Problem being solved and why it matters
Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS) is a common hormonal and metabolic condition, but its symptoms can vary significantly from person to person
Irregular menstrual cycles, acne, excessive hair growth, weight changes, sleep problems, and other symptoms may be overlooked or considered normal lifestyle-related changes.
The problem is therefore to develop a system that can help users recognize potential PCOS-related patterns earlier and understand their personal health trends, while directing them toward professional medical evaluation when appropriate.

2)Target users/stakeholders
Individuals experiencing irregular periods or other PCOS-related symptoms
Individuals already diagnosed with PCOS who want to monitor their symptoms and lifestyle

3)limitations of the existing approach
*Late recognition: PCOS symptoms are often overlooked or mistaken for normal menstrual/lifestyle changes.
  *Limited long-term tracking: They may not effectively connect menstrual cycles, symptoms, lifestyle, and     weight over time.
  *No clear pattern identification: Users often struggle to understand what factors may be associated with changes in their cycles or symptoms.

4)Proposed Solution & What Makes It Different
Develop a PCOS-focused health monitoring platform to track menstrual cycles, symptoms, BMI, physical activity, diet, sleep, and stress.
Use rule-based analysis to identify unusual patterns such as irregular cycles, significant weight changes, or prolonged gaps between periods.
Provide personalized lifestyle suggestions based on the user's recorded data.
Display graphs and trends to help users understand changes in their symptoms and menstrual patterns over time.
Include medication and appointment reminders for users already managing PCOS.
Generate a summary report that users can share with healthcare professionals.

What Makes It Different
Personalized: Recommendations are based on the user's own tracked data rather than generic advice.
Long-term monitoring: Connects menstrual, lifestyle, and symptom data over multiple months.
Early awareness: Helps users recognize concerning patterns and encourages timely medical consultation.

5)expected outcomes
*Increase awareness of potential PCOS-related symptoms
*Encourage earlier consultation with healthcare professionals
*Help users understand their menstrual and lifestyle patterns
*Provide users with a structured health history that can be discussed with healthcare professionals

8) how components interact
Interaction: User enters health data → JavaScript processes the information → predefined rules identify patterns → dashboard displays results → charts and basic recommendations are shown.

10)Expected Technical Challenges & Solutions
Incomplete or incorrect user data → Use input validation and required fields.
Medical accuracy → Use trusted medical guidelines and predefined rules.
Data storage → Use browser Local Storage for the prototype.
Limited development time → Prioritize the core features first.
Data privacy → Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information and keep data stored locally.
